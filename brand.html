What’s new

Inspired by our core values, the new identity captures the spirit of MINT and the people who build with us. It’s bolder, more technical, and a lot more fun.

Symbol

Our new mark is a pixel-built “m”: simple, distinctive, and made of the same blocks every 3D model starts from. It’s small enough to sit in a favicon and strong enough to carry a billboard.

Wordmark

The lowercase “mint” wordmark pairs with the symbol to feel direct and unfussy. No ornament, no noise. It stays legible at any size, in any color, on any background.

Color System

Red is our signal. We paired a hot red-orange with deep black and clean white to keep things high-contrast, confident, and impossible to miss. Use it loudly, or let it appear as a single spark.

Typography

Our typefaces are Geist and Geist Mono. Geist keeps our words clear and modern, and Geist Mono brings the developer’s eye for detail. Together they speak both design and code.

Visual Identity

Our imagery lives in the dark: Dither-ASCII, meshes, and light that resolve into form. It’s how we show what MINT actually does, turning raw points into something real.

Application

From the favicon in your browser tab to the badge around your neck at a conference, the new identity is rolling out across our site, docs, social, and product. You can expect it everywhere MINT shows up.

Looking ahead

We’re just getting started. We plan to keep shipping new model capabilities, faster generation, and more ways to plug MINT into the tools you already use, so it’s always the first thing you reach for when you need 3D. The look is new. The commitment isn’t. Our team of engineers, designers, and builders will keep making MINT faster, more reliable, and easier to use.
